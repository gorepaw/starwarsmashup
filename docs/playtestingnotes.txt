[ANSWERED] Speaking of level, how does exp work right now? give me a full rundown. do we need to reconcile a base system
with our new professions work?
  -> There is no character level: two separate things are called XP.
     (1) "XP" on the HUD is one running total on the character (characters.experience). Nothing reads it:
         it gates nothing and has no level.
         It grows from: kills of creatures and bandits (their archetype's Xp: worrt 20 ... ronto 160),
         quest rewards (Xp = in the quests: 60-200 now), and battle rewards (250 win / 150 draw / 100
         loss, +25 per kill, +60 per capture).
     (2) Each of the 31 professions has its own XP and level 1-100: 100 XP to go from 1 to 2, then 15 more
         for each level after, so level 25 is 6,540 XP, 50 is 22,540 and 100 is 82,665 (profession.cpp).
         Only 17 are live (4 armor, 5 weapon, 3 gathering, 5 crafting); the 14 specialist and companion
         ones wait on what they're about.
         It's earned by doing the thing: the held weapon's profession gets a creature/bandit kill's
         whole XP (a match kill is a flat 25), armor gets 1 per 10 damage taken, extracting is 15-40, a
         harvested corpse 28, salvaging and crafting their schematic's XP, work orders their reward.
         XP with no one profession's (quests, missions, battle results) goes to the loadout's slotted
         specialization professions, else its primary weapon's and its armor's, split evenly.
         Milestones are at 1/25/50/75/100 (certifications, gadgets, abilities); levels in between give
         small perks; a loadout's slotted professions apply their perks at the character's levels.
     So the professions ARE the leveling system (docs/PROFESSIONS.md says so: no SWG skill boxes, no base
     level). What's missing is that nothing says how strong a character is overall, for the things that
     want one: quest and enemy levels ("too low level" greying), who can use which area, the HUD's XP
     figure. Recommendation: keep no base level, and give those a number derived from the professions
     instead, e.g. a "combat level" = the higher of the held weapon's and the worn armor's professions
     (what you fight with), shown on the HUD in place of the bare XP total, with quests and creatures
     carrying a level against it (grey when 10 below, red when 5 or more above). Retire the XP total (or
     keep it as lifetime XP, a statistic). Say if you'd rather have a character level.

     the machinery exists; the missing part is the data.)
